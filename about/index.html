NOOR EVELYN RUSSELL

Drawn to places.
Moved by feeling.

I’m an independent hospitality and travel photographer, drawn to the intersection of architecture, travel, and the human experience of place.

I spent two years as a Community Partner at Palmaïa, a hotel recognized with Two MICHELIN Keys. Working within hospitality gave me an inside perspective on the experience of a thoughtfully considered stay. That experience informs the way I approach photographing a property: with attention to both its design and how it feels to be a guest.

My approach begins with curiosity: the way light moves through a room, the texture of a well-worn doorway, the sense of possibility in an unfamiliar landscape.

I’m interested in thoughtful design and cultural storytelling — in photographs that communicate not just how a place looks, but how it might feel to be there.
